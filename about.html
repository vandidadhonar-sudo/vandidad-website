We build AI operating systems for businesses that need more than a chatbot.

Vandidad Group is a technology company based in İzmir, Türkiye. We design and deploy custom AI agents and multi-agent operating systems that handle real commercial work — qualifying leads, holding sales conversations, and producing decision documents — in Persian, Turkish, and English.

Our systems run on Amazon Web Services. The founder is Mohammadhadi Bakhtzadeh.

What we build

AIOS — AI Operating System A multi-agent orchestration layer that gives a business a single intelligent front door. It routes a conversation across specialist agents, keeps context between sessions and channels, and hands the thread to WhatsApp or Telegram without the customer repeating themselves.
AIOS Twin — digital twin A conversational twin of a professional, deployed on their own domain. It holds a substantive consultation with a visitor, understands the visitor's situation, and produces a written dossier at the end. Live at vandidad.xyz.
Vanta AI Sales OS A bespoke sales operating system that automates qualification and follow-up across messaging channels, built per client rather than sold as a template.

Built on AWS

Our production infrastructure runs on Amazon Web Services. We use Amazon Bedrock for foundation model inference — primarily Anthropic Claude models through EU and US cross-region inference profiles — with AWS Lambda for agent execution and messaging integrations.
